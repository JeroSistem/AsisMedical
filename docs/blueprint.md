# **App Name**: MediTrack Pro

## Core Features:

- Patient Registration: Patient Registration: Securely register new patients with essential demographic information.
- EHR Creation: Electronic Health Record (EHR) Creation: Create and edit detailed electronic health records for each patient, including medical history, notes, and observations.
- Diagnostic and Treatment Log: Diagnostic and Treatment Log: Record diagnoses, treatment plans, and medication prescriptions within the EHR.
- Document Upload: Document Upload: Securely upload and attach relevant medical documents (e.g., lab results, imaging reports) to patient records.
- Advanced Search and Filtering: Advanced Search and Filtering: Quickly search and filter patient records based on various criteria (name, medical condition, date of birth, etc.).
- AI-Powered Summary: AI-Powered Summary Tool: Provide clinicians with concise summaries of patient records using a generative AI tool to quickly review key details of a patient's medical history and current status.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft blue (#64B5F6) for a professional and calming feel.
- Background color: Very light blue (#F0F8FF) to maintain a clean, clinical appearance.
- Accent color: Pale greenish-blue (#A7FFEB) to highlight important actions and notifications.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for a modern and easily readable interface.
- Use professional and clear icons to represent different medical terms and actions.
- Clean and structured layout with clear sections for patient information and medical history.
